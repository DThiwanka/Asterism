# Logo — Asterism VS Code Extension Icon

## Objective

Design the app icon for Asterism, a VS Code extension that draws interactive
maps of a codebase: functions and classes are nodes, and the calls between
them are connecting lines. Clicking a node traces what it calls and what
calls it.

An asterism is a small, recognizable pattern of stars, like the Big Dipper.
The icon should show code as a star pattern.

The icon must be recognizable at 42 px in the VS Code Extensions sidebar
and look sharp at 256 px on the Marketplace page.

---

# 1. Concept

Show a small star pattern of 4–6 stars joined by thin constellation lines,
like a star chart.

The main shape is built around three brighter stars forming a triangle,
inspired by the ⁂ asterism symbol. One or two smaller stars branch off
it, so it reads as a pattern rather than a plain triangle.

One star is the focus:
* the largest and brightest star
* has one outgoing line in blue and one incoming line in green

The other lines are soft and muted.

This mirrors the extension's main feature: tracing a function's calls.

---

# 2. Stars

* draw the stars as clean four-point sparkle shapes, or as bright round dots
  with a soft glow; use one style consistently
* vary the star sizes slightly (large, medium, small) for depth
* the stars must be solid and bold enough to survive shrinking
* do not use five-point cartoon stars

---

# 3. Style

* flat vector style
* simple geometric shapes
* consistent line weight, thick enough to survive shrinking
* rounded line caps
* no 3D, no bevels, no heavy shadows, no glossy effects
* only a soft glow around the stars

---

# 4. Colors

* Background: deep night-sky navy (#141B2D) in a rounded square tile
* Stars: warm white (#FFF6E0) with a hint of gold (#EE9D28) on the focus star
* Traced lines: blue (#3794FF) outgoing, green (#89D185) incoming
* Other lines: muted gray-blue (#5A6478)
* Optional: a few tiny faint background stars in the far corners

The icon must stand out on both white and dark backgrounds.

---

# 5. Composition

* square, 1:1
* centered, with even padding around the pattern
* rounded-square tile, corner radius about 22% of the width
* no more than 6 main stars

---

# 6. Do NOT Include

* any text, letters, or the name
* typographic asterisks (*) or the ⁂ character itself; use it only as
  inspiration for the triangle shape
* the VS Code logo or anything resembling it
* planets, moons, rockets, galaxies, or nebula clouds
* brains, robots, or generic AI sparkle symbols
* code symbols like </> or {}
* busy detail, thin hairlines, or tiny stars that disappear when shrunk
* photographic or realistic space textures

---

# Output

A single square icon, 1024×1024, flat vector look, on the navy tile.
Generate 4 variations:
1. triangle of three stars plus one branching star
2. triangle plus two branching stars
3. four-point sparkle stars instead of dots
4. the same pattern, simplified as much as possible for tiny sizes
